# The Hub — Content Queue

The publishing backlog for the daily Hub build routine (`gw-daily-hub-seo`).
Work **top-down within the current phase**. Check off (`- [x]`) every item published.
If an item is already covered by an existing page, check it off with `(covered by <slug>)`.

**Goal:** make gwhlds.com the page Google and the AI answer engines reach for when
someone is looking for a day trading community to join — and the education that
proves we deserve it.

---

## Strategy: why the phases are in this order

The instinct is to write "what is a candlestick" because it has huge volume. That is
the wrong first move. High-volume "what is" queries are dominated by Investopedia and
Babypips, convert almost nobody, and take years to outrank.

**Phase 1 is commercial-intent first.** Queries like "best day trading discord" have a
fraction of the volume but the searcher is *actively looking for what we sell*. They are
far easier to rank for, and they are the queries where an AI answer engine names two or
three communities — being one of those names is the whole game.

Phase 2+ builds the topical authority that makes Phase 1 pages credible and rankable.
Depth before breadth: own day trading completely before wandering into crypto.

**Cannibalization rule:** one keyword, one page. Before writing, check the slug list and
the queue — if another page already targets the phrase, link to it instead of competing.

## Refilling this queue — pull the next batch from the keyword map

This queue is a **working set of ~100 titles**. The full keyword inventory — head
("regular") keywords and long-tail spokes for the entire trading knowledge domain,
sixteen clusters deep — lives in **[`keyword-map.md`](keyword-map.md)**.

**Trigger:** fewer than **20 unchecked items** left here.

**Then:**
1. Open the **Batch ledger** at the bottom of `keyword-map.md` and take the next batch row.
2. Convert its `MAP` spokes into real page titles — the way a person wants the question
   answered, not the raw keyword.
3. Drop any title that duplicates a live page's target phrase. Check the slug list first —
   **one keyword, one page**, always.
4. Append the ~100 titles to the **end of the relevant phase** below, never the top.
   The order encodes the strategy.
5. Flip the batch row to *In flight*; mark the previous batch *Done*.
6. Report which batch was pulled and what was dropped for cannibalization.

This is the "every 100 pages, another 100" loop. At 3 pages per run, one batch is
roughly 33 run-days.

**If the map itself runs low**, refill it from Search Console (queries ranking 5–20 —
the fastest wins), Google PAA and autocomplete on each head keyword, Reddit
r/Daytrading, r/Forex and r/FuturesTrading, gaps in clusters with fewer than five live
spokes, and modifier crosses — but only where the answer genuinely differs.
